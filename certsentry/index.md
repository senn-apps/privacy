# CertSentry Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

CertSentry stores your certifications, renewal dates, reminders, and attached files locally on your device. It does not require an account.

## Data Collection

CertSentry does **not** collect, transmit, or share personal data.

## Certifications and Settings

Certification details you enter — including names, issuers, certificate numbers, issue and expiry dates, renewal requirements, and reminder preferences — are stored in a local database inside the app sandbox. Settings such as the notification switch and onboarding status are stored locally with the app.

## Attachments

PDF and image files you attach to a certification are copied into the app sandbox (`Application Support/Attachments`) on your device. They remain there unless you delete the app.

## Notifications

Renewal reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. CertSentry does not use remote push notifications and does not send your certificate data to a server.

## Calendar Export

If you choose to export a certification to your calendar, CertSentry asks for calendar access and writes a single event with the certification name and expiry date to your device's default calendar. Turning the export off removes that event. CertSentry does not read your existing calendar events.

## Data Export

You can export your certification data as a JSON file. The export is saved only where you choose on your device; CertSentry does not upload it.

## Purchases

CertSentry is purchased through the Apple App Store as a one-time paid download. Apple processes payments and transaction information according to Apple's privacy policies. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## Third Parties

CertSentry does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
