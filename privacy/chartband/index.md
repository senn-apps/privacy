# Chartband Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Chartband stores projects, counters, notes, and imported pattern files locally on your device. It does not require an account.

## Data Collection

Chartband does **not** collect, transmit, or share personal data.

## Imported Patterns

Pattern files you import are copied into the app sandbox (`Application Support/Patterns`) on your device and remain there unless you delete them or the app.

## Row Counters and Notes

Counters, notes, and row highlight positions are stored locally with the app.

## Purchases

Chartband Pro is purchased through the Apple App Store. Apple processes payments and transaction information according to Apple's privacy policies.

## Third Parties

Chartband does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
