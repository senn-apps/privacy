# FamDose Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

FamDose is a medication reminder app for older adults and their families. It keeps your health information on your device. It does not require an account.

## Data Collection

FamDose does **not** collect, transmit, or share personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no cloud sync, and no server. All medication names, schedules, confirmation records, and family contacts stay in local storage on your device.

## Medication and Family Data

Medication names, doses, times, and reminder preferences you enter are stored in a local database inside the app sandbox. Dose confirmation records — when a dose was confirmed and how (button, voice, or photo) — are stored there as well. Family contacts you enter for one-tap calling (name, relationship, phone number, and email address) are stored locally too. Preferences such as text size and reminder settings are stored with the app.

## Confirmation Photos

If you choose to confirm a dose with a photo, the photo is saved only in the app's private sandbox on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

## Voice and Camera Access

Voice confirmation uses on-device speech recognition. Your voice is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded. Camera and photo library access are used only to attach an optional confirmation photo. You can deny these permissions at any time in iOS Settings and still use the big confirmation button.

## Notifications

FamDose sends reminders at the medication times you set. These are local notifications, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. FamDose does not use remote push notifications and does not send your data to a server.

## Data Export and Sharing

The weekly report and JSON export features create a file that you choose to share using the iOS share sheet. FamDose does not send anything on your behalf — sharing happens only when you tap Share and choose a destination.

## Purchases

FamDose is a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. Apple processes payments and transaction information according to Apple's privacy policies. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## Children

FamDose is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Third Parties

FamDose does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not sell, share, or disclose any data.

## Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
